# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes agricultural performance across different seasons (Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid) using data analytics techniques in Python. It investigates the impact of environmental conditions, farming practices, and resource management on crop yield and economic outcomes.

## Objectives
- Investigate seasonal variations in crop yield and total production.
- Analyze environmental drivers (rainfall, temperature, soil moisture).
- Evaluate water efficiency across irrigation methods (Drip, Sprinkler, Flood, Rainfed).
- Assess cost-revenue structures and identify profit-driving crops.
- Examine seasonal pest and disease risk dynamics.

## Tech Stack
- **Language:** Python 3
- **Libraries:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Environment:** Google Colab / Jupyter Notebook

## Key Insights
1. **Irrigation Efficiency:** Drip and Sprinkler systems demonstrated significantly higher water efficiency compared to Flood irrigation.
2. **Profit Margins:** High-value commercial crops (Chilli, Sugarcane) yield strong net profits when input costs are optimized.
3. **Pest Dynamics:** Kharif season recorded higher pest risks due to elevated humidity and monsoon rainfall.
